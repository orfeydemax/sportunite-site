# ChatGPT Prompts for Product Managers

This reference file contains a collection of 45+ prompts for various Product Management tasks, organized by category.

## Business Models

### 1. Business Model Canvas
**Context:** Create a comprehensive business model canvas for a new product.
**Prompt:**
> Generate a business canvas model for a [Product Description] that helps [Target Audience] [Value Proposition].

## Product Validation

### 2. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
**Context:** Estimate the market size for a new product.
**Prompt:**
> Act as a Product Manager. Assume TAM is Total Addressable Market, SAM is Serviceable Available Market and SOM is Serviceable Obtainable Market. How should I go about estimating the TAM, SAM and SOM for a [Product Description]? Please give examples of research sources I should check out.

## Product Ideation

### 3. Expanding Product Lines
**Context:** Identify adjacent product opportunities.
**Prompt:**
> I'm a Product Manager of a [Current Product Description]. I'm already monetizing this service well and now want to branch off to other areas. What adjacent product or services can I offer that leverages my strengths & can quickly turn a profit?

### 4. Aiding Product Ideation
**Context:** Brainstorm new product ideas in a specific domain.
**Prompt:**
> Suggest 3 tech product ideas I could pursue in the [Industry/Domain] that could turn a profit in less than 2 years. Describe the idea and also share how it will make money.

## Product Vision

### 5. Product 1-Pager
**Context:** Align the team with a clear product vision.
**Prompt:**
> I'm a Product Manager planning to build a [New Product Description]. Write a 1-pager that I can share with my engineering & design team to explain the product vision & the product components that will most likely be involved.

### 6. Amazon Press Release (Working Backwards)
**Context:** Define the product vision using the Amazon backwards approach.
**Prompt:**
> Assume you’re building a [Product Description]. You’re launching a new feature that will [Feature Description]. Using Amazon’s “Working Backwards” model, write a press release for this product feature. Include some impressive metrics. Give it a catchy title.

## Product Strategy

### 7. Product Strategy (DHM Model)
**Context:** Draft a strategy using Gibson Biddle's DHM model.
**Prompt:**
> Gibson Biddle has a product strategy framework called the DHM Model. D stands for delight, H stands for hard-to-copy, and M stands for margin-enhancing. In short, the DHM model aims to answer: “How will your product delight customers, in hard-to-copy, margin-enhancing ways?”. Now, act as a Product Manager for a [Product Description]. Give me a product strategy for this product using Biddle's DHM model.

## Positioning

### 8. Competitive Intelligence
**Context:** Compare your product to competitors.
**Prompt:**
> I'm developing a [Product Description]. We are serving the [Target Market]. Our unique value prop is [Value Proposition]. Can you write a competitive intelligence report comparing us to other players that I can share in an investor brief?

## Discovery

### 9. Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Questions
**Context:** Uncover user needs and motivations.
**Prompt:**
> Assume I'm a Product Manager for a [Product Description]. I want to conduct some consumer research to figure out my user's jobs-to-be-done. What personas should I target and then what questions should I be asking them to elicit their job-to-be-done? Take inspiration from the book "Mom Test".

### 10. Drafting Survey Questions
**Context:** Create a survey for user feedback.
**Prompt:**
> Using guidelines mentioned in Teresa Torres' book "Continuous Discovery Habits", formulate a survey to capture feedback for a [Product Description].

### 11. Outreach Emails for Interviews
**Context:** Invite users to user research interviews.
**Prompt:**
> Write an email that I can send to users of my [Product Description] inviting them to a short [Time]-minute feedback call on ways we can improve their experience. Incentivize them with a [Incentive].

### 12. Generating Personas
**Context:** Create detailed user personas.
**Prompt:**
> Generate 3 fleshed out persona profiles for a [Product Description] that primarily serves the [Target Market]?

### 13. Opportunity Solution Trees
**Context:** Map opportunities to desired outcomes.
**Prompt:**
> Act as a product manager for a [Product Description]. Assume that the desired outcome you’re working for is [Desired Outcome]. Craft an opportunity tree based on Teresa Torres’ book, Continuous Discovery Habits, against this desired outcome.

## Design Thinking

### 14. Customer Journey Maps
**Context:** Visualize the user journey.
**Prompt:**
> Create a sample customer journey map for a [Product Description] in a tabular format.

### 15. Design Sprint Brief
**Context:** Plan a design sprint.
**Prompt:**
> I'm a Product Manager that's running a design sprint for my [Product Description]. The problem we're trying to solve: "[Problem Statement]". Write a memo explaining to my team what we'll be doing on each day of the design sprint. Give a few examples of what the solutions can look like.

## Product Roadmaps

### 16. Roadmap Ideas (Strategy Aligned)
**Context:** Generate roadmap items based on a specific strategy.
**Prompt:**
> I’m working for a product that’s a direct competitor of [Competitor]. The product strategy is [Strategy Description]. Give me a list of roadmap ideas aligned with this strategy.

### 17. Roadmap Drafting (Now/Next/Later)
**Context:** Create a simple roadmap.
**Prompt:**
> I just joined as a new product manager for a [Product Description]. Can you draft a sample roadmap for this product? Limit the roadmap items to 10. Categorize them under the “Now”, “Next”, “Later” labels.

## Solutionizing

### 18. Buy vs. Build Decision
**Context:** Evaluate whether to build in-house or buy a solution.
**Prompt:**
> Act as the Product Manager of a [Product Description]. You are planning to build a feature [Feature Description]. This requires [Requirement]. You have two choices: 1- you can integrate with a off-the-shelf solution OR 2- you can implement the module with your in-house engineering team. Compare both choices.

### 19. Understanding Tech Jargon
**Context:** Explain technical concepts simply.
**Prompt:**
> I just started as a Product Manager for a [Product Description]. The product uses [Technical Term] which I don’t understand well. Can you explain this in simple terms? Give me some example usages of this when I’m writing a spec.

### 20. Pros & Cons Analysis
**Context:** Weigh options for a product decision.
**Prompt:**
> I'm a Product Manager for a [Product Description]. We are launching a [Feature Description]. The management is debating [Decision Point]. Give arguments for & against each option.

## Prioritization

### 21. Kano Model Classification
**Context:** Classify features using the Kano model.
**Prompt:**
> I’m building a [Product Description]. The product team uses the KANO model to prioritize features. Can you give me examples of features that would qualify as “basic”, “excitement” and “performance” according to Kano? Tabulate this.

### 22. Weighted-Value Prioritization
**Context:** Prioritize features using a weighted model.
**Prompt:**
> I’m building a [Product Description]. I have the following 4 features: [List Features]. How would you prioritize them based on weighted-value models?
